## What it is
A Ration Card allows a household to purchase subsidized food grains under
the Public Distribution System (PDS) / National Food Security Act (NFSA).

## Documents typically required
- Identity proof of head of household
- Address proof
- Family details (Aadhaar of all members, in most states)
- Income certificate, in some states, to determine card category (AAY /
  Priority / General)

## General process
1. Apply online through the state Food & Civil Supplies Department portal,
   or in person at the local ration/taluk office.
2. Local verification of the household may be conducted.
3. Card issued/linked digitally in most states; category (AAY, Priority,
   etc.) determines subsidy level.

## Fee
Nominal or free depending on the state — check the relevant Food & Civil
Supplies portal.

## Where to apply
State Food & Civil Supplies Department portal, or local ration office.
