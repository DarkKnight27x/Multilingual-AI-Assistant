## What it is
Old-age and widow pension schemes provide a monthly financial benefit to
eligible senior citizens and widows, generally under the National Social
Assistance Programme (NSAP) at the central level, often supplemented by
state-specific schemes with their own eligibility and amounts.

## Documents typically required
- Age proof (for old-age pension)
- Death certificate of husband (for widow pension)
- Identity and address proof
- Income certificate / BPL card, as eligibility criteria typically include
  an income threshold
- Bank account details for direct benefit transfer

## General process
1. **Always check eligibility first on the official myScheme portal** —
   exact scheme names, income thresholds, and pension amounts vary by
   state and are updated periodically; do not assume specifics without
   checking.
2. Apply through the state Social Welfare Department portal, or in person
   at the local Tahsildar / Panchayat / municipal office.
3. Verification of eligibility (age, income, residency) is conducted.
4. Approved pension is disbursed monthly, typically via direct bank
   transfer.

## Fee
No application fee is typically charged for pension schemes.

## Where to apply
State Social Welfare Department portal, or local Tahsildar/Panchayat
office. **Always verify current scheme details on myScheme first.**
