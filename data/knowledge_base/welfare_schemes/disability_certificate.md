## What it is
A Disability Certificate certifies the type and percentage of disability of
a person, issued under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
It's required for reservation in education/employment, tax benefits,
concessions, and disability pension.

## Documents typically required
- Identity and address proof
- Recent photographs
- Medical records related to the disability
- Aadhaar (linked to the UDID — Unique Disability ID — system in most
  states)

## General process
1. Apply online through the UDID (Unique Disability ID) portal, or at the
   nearest designated government hospital / district hospital with a
   Medical Board for disability assessment.
2. A Medical Board evaluates the applicant and determines the disability
   percentage and category.
3. Certificate (and UDID card) is issued after assessment.

## Fee
Generally free at government hospitals — verify with the local
authority/hospital.

## Where to apply
UDID portal, or the district's Medical Board / designated government
hospital.
