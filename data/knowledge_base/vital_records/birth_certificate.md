## What it is
A birth certificate is the official record of a child's birth, issued under
the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969. It is required for school
admission, passport applications, and many government services.

## Documents typically required
- Hospital-issued birth report (if born in a hospital) or a self-declaration
  from the head of the household (if born at home)
- Parents' identity proof (Aadhaar / voter ID / passport)
- Address proof of the parents
- Marriage certificate of parents (in some states)

## General process
1. Birth must be reported to the local registrar (usually the municipal
   corporation, municipality, or gram panchayat) within 21 days of birth.
2. If reported within 21 days, registration is usually free.
3. If reported after 21 days but within 30 days, a late fee and/or an
   affidavit may be required, varying by state.
4. If reported after one year, registration typically requires an order from
   a magistrate or the district registrar.
5. Apply online via the state's e-District portal or the CRS (Civil
   Registration System) portal, or in person at the local registrar's office.

## Fee
Registration within 21 days is generally free. Late registration fees vary
by state — check the relevant state e-District portal for exact amounts.
Never assume a fixed fee without checking the source.

## Where to apply
Local Registrar of Births & Deaths (municipal corporation / municipality /
panchayat office), or online through the state e-District portal.
