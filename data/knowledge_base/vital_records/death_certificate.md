## What it is
Official record of a death, required for settling insurance claims,
inheritance, pension transfer, and closing bank accounts.

## Documents typically required
- Hospital death report (if death occurred in a hospital) or the attending
  doctor's certificate
- Deceased's identity proof (Aadhaar / voter ID)
- Applicant's identity and address proof
- Cremation/burial ground receipt, where applicable

## General process
1. Death must be reported to the local registrar within 21 days.
2. Registration within 21 days is generally free.
3. Late registration (after 21 days, up to 1 year) usually needs a
   delay-condonation affidavit and a small fee.
4. After 1 year, registration typically requires a magistrate's order.
5. Apply via the local registrar's office or the state e-District / CRS
   portal.

## Fee
Free within 21 days in most states; late fees vary — verify with the state
e-District portal.

## Where to apply
Local Registrar of Births & Deaths, or the state e-District portal.
