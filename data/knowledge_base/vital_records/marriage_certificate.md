## What it is
A Marriage Certificate is legal proof of marriage, issued either under the
Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 or the Special Marriage Act, 1954 (for
inter-religious or civil marriages). Required for passport applications,
visa processes, name changes, and joint property/bank matters.

## Documents typically required
- Age proof of both spouses (must meet legal minimum age: 21 for groom,
  18 for bride)
- Address proof of both spouses
- Passport-size photographs
- Two witnesses with their identity proof
- Marriage invitation card or priest's certificate, in some states (for
  Hindu Marriage Act registration of an already-solemnized marriage)

## General process
1. **Hindu Marriage Act route** (for marriages already solemnized): apply
   to the local Sub-Registrar / Marriage Registrar for registration.
2. **Special Marriage Act route** (for new civil marriages or
   inter-religious marriages): give a notice of intended marriage to the
   Marriage Officer at least 30 days before the marriage; a 30-day
   objection period follows before the marriage can be solemnized and
   registered.
3. Many states now allow online appointment booking via the state
   e-District / Sub-Registrar portal.

## Fee
Nominal registration fee — varies by state; check the relevant e-District
or Sub-Registrar portal.

## Where to apply
Office of the Sub-Registrar / Marriage Registrar in the district where
either spouse resides, or via the state e-District portal.

## Official portals
- Respective state e-District / Registration Department portal
