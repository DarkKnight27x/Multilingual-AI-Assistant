## Tamil Nadu specific process
In Tamil Nadu, birth registration and certificate issuance is handled
through the Tamil Nadu e-Sevai centres and the TNeGA CRS portal.

## Where to apply
- Online: TNeGA Civil Registration portal, or via the tnesevai.tn.gov.in
  e-Sevai portal
- In person: Nearest e-Sevai Maiyam (CSC) or the local municipal /
  corporation office (Registrar of Births & Deaths)

## Fee
Free if registered within 21 days of birth. Late registration attracts a
fee that varies by delay period — confirm current amount on the portal
before applying.
