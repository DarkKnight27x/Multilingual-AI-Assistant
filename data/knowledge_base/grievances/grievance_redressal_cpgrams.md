## What it is
CPGRAMS (Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System) is the
online platform for citizens to lodge grievances against central and state
government departments regarding service delivery, corruption, or delays.

## Documents typically required
- Identity proof (for registration on the portal)
- Any supporting documents relevant to the grievance (application
  reference numbers, correspondence, receipts, etc.)

## General process
1. Register/log in on the CPGRAMS portal.
2. Select the concerned Ministry/Department/State and submit the grievance
   with relevant details and supporting documents.
3. A unique registration number is generated to track the status.
4. The concerned department is required to respond within a defined
   timeframe (commonly referenced as around 30 days, though this can vary
   by department — check the portal for current SLA details).
5. If unsatisfied, an appeal can be filed within the portal.

## Fee
Free.

## Where to apply
https://pgportal.gov.in, or the respective state's grievance portal where
one exists separately.
