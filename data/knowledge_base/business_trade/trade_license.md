## What it is
A Trade License is issued by the local municipal corporation/municipality
and permits a business to operate a specific trade at a specific location
within that jurisdiction. It's separate from GST registration or Udyam
registration and is primarily about local civic compliance (health,
safety, zoning).

## Documents typically required
- Identity and address proof of the applicant
- Proof of ownership or rental/lease agreement of the business premises
- Site/layout plan of the premises, in some cases
- NOC from the fire department, in some categories of trade
- PAN of the business

## General process
1. Apply through the local municipal corporation's online portal or
   Citizen Service Centre, or in person at the municipal office.
2. An inspection of the premises may be conducted depending on the trade
   category (e.g., food establishments typically need a health inspection).
3. License is issued upon approval, and typically needs to be renewed
   annually.

## Fee
Varies significantly by municipality and trade category — check the local
municipal corporation's portal for the current fee schedule.

## Where to apply
Local Municipal Corporation / Municipality, online portal or Citizen
Service Centre.

## Official portals
- Respective municipal corporation portal (varies by city)
