## What it is
An Income Certificate certifies a person's or family's annual income,
issued by the Revenue Department. It is commonly required for education
scholarships, fee concessions, and various welfare scheme applications.

## Documents typically required
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, voter ID)
- Address proof
- Salary slip / self-employment income declaration / Form 16, as applicable
- Ration card (in some states, as a supporting document)

## General process
1. Apply online through the state e-District portal, or in person at the
   Tahsildar / Revenue office / Common Service Centre.
2. A local enquiry (via VAO or Revenue Inspector) may be conducted for
   self-employed applicants without documented income.
3. Certificate is issued after verification, usually valid for one year.

## Fee
Nominal fee, varies by state — check the relevant e-District portal.

## Processing time
Typically 7–15 working days, but varies by state and verification needs.

## Where to apply
Tahsildar / Revenue office, or state e-District portal.

## Official portals
- Respective state e-District portal
