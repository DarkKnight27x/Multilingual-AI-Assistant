## What it is
A Domicile (Residence) Certificate certifies that a person is a resident of
a particular state. It's required for state government job applications,
education quotas, and some scholarships.

## Documents typically required
- Proof of residence (ration card, voter ID, utility bills) covering the
  minimum residency period required by the state (commonly several years,
  varies by state — check local rules)
- Identity proof
- School leaving certificate or birth certificate, in some states

## General process
1. Apply through the state e-District portal or at the Tahsildar / Revenue
   office.
2. Local verification of residence history may be conducted.
3. Certificate issued after verification.

## Fee
Nominal fee, varies by state — check the relevant e-District portal.

## Where to apply
Tahsildar / Revenue office, or state e-District portal.

## Official portals
- Respective state e-District portal
