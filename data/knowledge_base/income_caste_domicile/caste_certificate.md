## What it is
A Caste Certificate certifies that a person belongs to a Scheduled Caste
(SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), or Other Backward Class (OBC), as officially
recognized in that state. It is required for reservation benefits in
education, employment, and welfare schemes.

## Documents typically required
- Identity and address proof
- Proof of caste of a blood relative (father/grandfather), such as an
  earlier issued caste certificate, school records, or revenue records
- Self-declaration affidavit, in some states

## General process
1. Apply through the state e-District portal, at the Tahsildar / Revenue
   office, or through the concerned Social Welfare Department.
2. Verification of caste records, often through local revenue officials.
3. Certificate issued after verification — validity and renewal rules
   vary by state.

## Fee
Nominal fee, varies by state — check the relevant e-District portal.

## Where to apply
Tahsildar / Revenue office or Social Welfare Department, or state
e-District portal.

## Official portals
- Respective state e-District portal
