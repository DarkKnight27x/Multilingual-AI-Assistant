## What it is
Mutation is the process of updating land/property revenue records to
reflect a change in ownership — after sale, inheritance, gift, or partition.
It does not itself confer legal title but is important evidentiary record
for tax and future transactions.

## Documents typically required
- Sale deed / gift deed / will / legal heir certificate (depending on how
  ownership changed)
- Previous property tax receipts
- Identity and address proof of the applicant
- Encumbrance certificate (in some states)

## General process
1. Apply to the local municipal corporation / panchayat (for urban/rural
   property tax records) or the Tahsildar / Revenue office (for land
   records), depending on the state's structure.
2. Many states now offer online mutation applications through the state
   land records or e-District portal (e.g., Bhulekh, Dharani, Bhoomi —
   names vary by state).
3. Field verification may be conducted before the mutation is approved.

## Fee
Varies by state and property value — check the relevant state land records
portal.

## Where to apply
Local municipal body or Revenue/Tahsildar office, or the state's online
land records portal.

## Official portals
- Respective state land records portal (varies by state)
