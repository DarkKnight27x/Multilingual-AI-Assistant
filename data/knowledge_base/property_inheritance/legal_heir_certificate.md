## What it is
A Legal Heir Certificate identifies the rightful heirs of a deceased person.
It is used for transferring property, pension, provident fund, insurance
claims, and other benefits where a succession certificate is not mandatory.

## Documents typically required
- Death certificate of the deceased
- Applicant's identity and address proof
- Proof of relationship with the deceased (ration card, family register,
  Aadhaar family linkage, etc.)
- Address proof of the deceased at time of death

## General process
1. Apply at the Tahsildar / Revenue Divisional Officer (RDO) office in the
   deceased's last residential jurisdiction, or via the state e-District
   portal where available.
2. A local Village Administrative Officer (VAO) or Revenue Inspector
   verification/enquiry is usually conducted.
3. Certificate is typically issued after verification, listing all legal
   heirs.

## Fee
Nominal application fee; exact amount varies by state — check the relevant
e-District portal.

## Processing time
Typically a few weeks, depending on the verification process — varies by
state and district workload.

## Where to apply
Tahsildar office / Revenue Department, or state e-District portal.

## Official portals
